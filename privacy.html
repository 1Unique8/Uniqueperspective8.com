Privacy Policy

What we collect when you visit, write, or order — and what we do not do with it.

Who we are

This site is uniqueperspective8.com, operated by Morgan Sugg, Unique Perspective, British Columbia, Canada. Orders may also be placed through shop.uniqueperspective8.com.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to answer you and to complete a sale:

  • Name, email, shipping address, and phone number when you order or contact us.
  • Payment details handled by the shop’s payment processor. We do not store full card numbers on this website.
  • Messages you send through the contact form or email.
  • If comments are enabled, the text of the comment, your IP address, and browser user agent, used to limit spam.
  • Basic technical data such as pages visited and device type, through cookies or similar tools described in our Cookie Policy.

Why we use it

To fill orders, ship packages, reply to questions, keep records required for a small business, and keep the site working. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Who else may see it

Carriers see what they need to deliver a parcel. The shop platform and payment processor see what they need to take payment. Email and hosting providers see what passes through their systems. We use service providers only to run the business, not to build marketing lists for sale.

How long we keep it

Order and contact records are kept as long as needed to finish the sale, handle returns, and meet bookkeeping or legal duties, then deleted or archived with ordinary care. You may ask what we hold about you.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct, or delete personal information we hold, or withdraw consent where the law allows. Some records must be kept for a time (for example, a completed order). Browser settings control most cookies; blocking them may affect checkout or forms.

Children

This site is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Privacy questions: questions4uniqueperspective@gmail.com or payments4uniqueperspective@gmail.com.

This page describes our practice. It is not legal advice. If our practice changes, we will update this page.
