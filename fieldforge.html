Fieldforge

Fieldforge is Unique Perspective 8’s specimen intake progressive web app. It is built for geological provenance and lapidary metrology: field capture, workshop notes, diagnostic tests, a specimen ledger with lineage, printable labels, and export.

Open Fieldforge ↗ Source on GitHub ↗

What it does

  • Station A — Field Mobile: locality selector (Similkameen, Hedley, Okanagan, Sumas, Princeton, or custom), GPS capture, dual photo upload (rough and slab), weight, size, and visual description.
  • Station B — Workshop: cabochon stencil and metrology notes, kerf guidance, and dimensional tracking after slabbing.
  • Diagnostic protocol: Mohs, specific gravity, streak, acid, UV, magnet, candling, and soundness, with notes.
  • Master specimen ledger: dual photos, weights, dimensions, tests, appraisals, and local browser storage.
  • Assistant: a rules-based copilot that can suggest possible ID, a rough value range, and disposition, plus SG formula and kerf reminders.
  • Export: JSON download, optional Google Sheets webhook, and printable specimen drawer labels.

How it runs

Fieldforge is a single-file progressive web app. Open it in a modern browser or use the hosted app. It is mobile-first and offline-capable for field use. All data stays local in the browser unless you export it.

The live app is hosted at fieldforge.uniqueperspective8.com. Specimens recorded there can later support educational pages, the Know the cut cabinet, or shop provenance stories.
