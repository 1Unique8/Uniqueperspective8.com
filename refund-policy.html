Refund & Returns Policy

Your satisfaction matters to us

Our refund and returns policy lasts 14 days from delivery. If 14 days have passed since delivery, we cannot offer you a full refund or exchange.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused and in the same condition that you received it. It must also be in the original packaging. We require a receipt or proof of purchase.

Custom jewelry orders are final sale unless there is a defect. One-of-a-kind studio pieces and educational specimens are sold as described on their provenance card; natural variation in color, size, and finish is not a defect.

We only replace items if they are defective or damaged on arrival, or if we sent the wrong item.

Once your return is received and inspected, we will email you to confirm we have it and to tell you whether the refund is approved. If approved, the refund is returned to your original payment method within 10 business days of our receiving the return.

To start a return, email customer_service@uniqueperspective8.com with your order number. Mail the item to:

Unique Perspective 8
7104 Highway 97
Oliver BC V0H 1T7
Canada

You are responsible for return shipping costs. Outbound shipping charges are non-refundable. If you receive a refund, return shipping will not be reimbursed, and any non-refundable outbound shipping already paid may be deducted from the refund.

Use a trackable service for returns. We cannot refund an item we have not received.

If the item was marked as a gift when purchased and shipped directly to you, an approved return is issued as store credit. If it was not marked as a gift, the refund goes to the original payer.

Questions about a return? Email customer_service@uniqueperspective8.com or contact us.
