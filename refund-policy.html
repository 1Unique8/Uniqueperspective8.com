Refund & Returns Policy

Returns, refunds, and cancellations for Unique Perspective 8

We accept returns within 14 days from delivery. Items must be unused and in their original condition and packaging.

To start a return, email support@uniqueperspective8.com with your order number. Mail the item to:

Unique Perspective 8
7104 Highway 97
Oliver, BC V0H 1T7
Canada

Unique Perspective 8 pays return shipping on all returns. There are no prepaid return labels; you ship the return yourself, and we reimburse the return postage with your refund.

The original outbound shipping charge is refunded only if the item arrived damaged, defective, or wrong.

Custom jewelry orders are final sale unless there is a defect, as stated in our Policies. No other items are non-returnable.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days of our receiving the return.

You may cancel an order until it has been produced or shipped. Email support@uniqueperspective8.com with your order number.

We reply to support email within 2 days.

Questions about a return? Email support@uniqueperspective8.com or contact us.
