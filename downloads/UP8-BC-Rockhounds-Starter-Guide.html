2. Three field tests

  • Wet test. Water simulates a polish and shows banding.
  • Knife test. Scratch = calcite/limestone (Mohs 3–4). No mark = jasper/quartz (Mohs 6.5–7).
  • Flashlight edge test. Glow = chalcedony/agate. Opaque = chert/jasper.

3. Field code

  • Verify open Crown land on Mineral Titles Online. Do not collect on active claims or private land without permission.
  • This is the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the Syilx Okanagan Nation. Leave artifacts untouched (BC Heritage Conservation Act).
  • Leave no trace. Do not cut unstable riverbanks. Backfill hand test holes.
