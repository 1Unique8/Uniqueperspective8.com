The BC Free Miner Stewardship Standard

Dug by Hand. Crafted by Heart. | Fall 7, Stand 8.

This paper is the long form of how Unique Perspective 8 collects, verifies, and documents stone. It is not a mine permit, not a claim, and not an assay certificate.

Transparent provenance

Document from river corridor to bench. If a sentence cannot be tied to a date, a place, and a method, it does not belong on a listing.

Recreational vs commercial

Governed by the BC Mineral Tenure Act. Recreational collecting is personal and small. An FMC is standing for small-scale commercial collecting and lawful prospecting — not a mine permit. Hand tools only.

Mineral Titles Online

Check Crown land, active claims, private property, and parklands before the first hole. Respect existing tenure.

Territory and heritage

Unceded territory of the Syilx Okanagan Nation. Heritage sites and fossils stay in place. Unexpected find: leave it, record GPS, notify authorities.

Riparian protection

Pack out waste. Backfill test pits. Leave no lasting scar on riverbanks.

Provenance card

Permanent field ID at discovery. Card carries date, location, formation, FMC number when applicable, and artisan signature. Studio work is recycled 925 sterling without resins or coatings.
