Studio Guidebook

Handmade sterling and gold pieces are formed in the studio. They may contain both metal recovered by hand from lawful ground and purchased metal melted with it. We do not claim a piece is entirely field-recovered unless that batch was kept separate.

1. Sentences

Copper claim: studio handmade only. Gold/silver: mixed unless recorded separate. FMC: standing, not a mine. Privacy: do not sell personal information.

2–3. Ethics and field work

Separate means purchased stock stayed out of the pot. An FMC is a yearly key. Parks, private land, titles, and Indigenous lands are not implied.

4–5. Purity and alloy ID

Stamp only a number you can defend. Identify alloy by look, magnet, marks, then test. Plating is not the core.

6–7. Assay

Acid and XRF sort. Fire assay (fusion, cupellation, parting) can support a quality mark.

8–11. Privacy, site, checklist, refusal

See privacy.html and ethics.html. If the object cannot survive the question, cut the line.
