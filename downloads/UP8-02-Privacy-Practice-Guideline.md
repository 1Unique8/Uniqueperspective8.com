# Privacy Practice

*Collect what the sale or the reply requires. Do not sell the person.*

We collect what we need to answer you and to complete a sale. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

## Who we are
uniqueperspective8.com is operated by Morgan Sugg, Unique Perspective, British Columbia, Canada. Orders may also run through shop.uniqueperspective8.com.

## What we may collect
- Name, email, shipping address, and phone when someone orders or writes.
- Payment details handled by the shop's payment processor. Full card numbers are not stored on uniqueperspective8.com.
- Messages through the contact form or email.
- If comments are enabled: comment text, IP address, and browser user agent, to limit spam.
- Basic technical data through cookies described on cookies.html.

## Use
To fill orders, ship parcels, answer questions, keep ordinary books, and keep the site working. Carriers, the shop platform, the payment processor, and hosting or email providers see what their job requires.

## Retention and requests
Keep records as long as the sale, a return, or bookkeeping requires. A person may ask what is held, and may ask for correction or deletion where the law allows.
