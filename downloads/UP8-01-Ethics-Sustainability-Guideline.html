Ethics & Sustainability

The public sentence and the bench habit must be the same sentence.

Handmade sterling and gold pieces are formed in the studio. They may contain both metal recovered by hand from lawful ground and purchased metal melted with it. We do not claim a piece is entirely field-recovered unless that batch was kept separate.

Copper

Recycled copper wire is used on handmade studio work only. That claim does not travel with drop-shipped items.

Gold and silver

Studio pieces may mix field-recovered metal and purchased stock in one melt. Entirely field-recovered only if that batch was kept separate and recorded.

Free Miner Certificate

Standing to seek titles and do limited exploration. Not a mine permit. Not proof that every shop object came from the field.

Separate-batch rule

  1. Purchased stock did not enter the pot.
  2. Date, source note, and a mark travel with the piece.
  3. If only memory exists, use the mixed-metal sentence.
