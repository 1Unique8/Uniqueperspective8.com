Our Mission

We believe rare materials deserve thoughtful presentation, ethical sourcing, and intentional design.

How the metal is made

Handmade sterling and gold pieces are formed in the studio. They may contain both metal recovered by hand from lawful ground and purchased metal melted with it. A piece is not called entirely field-recovered unless that batch was kept separate. Recycled copper wire is used on handmade studio work. Drop-shipped items are labeled and sit outside these studio claims.

Morgan holds a Free Miner Certificate in British Columbia. That certificate is a legal standing for exploration, not a claim that every object in the shop came from a mine. The full standard lives on the Ethics & Sustainability page.
