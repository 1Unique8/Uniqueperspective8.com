How we use cookies on our website.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences and improve your experience.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar tools to keep the site working, remember basic preferences, and understand which pages are visited. Some are set by this website. Others may be set by the shop host, payment processor, or analytics tools when you use those features. We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

This page does not list every third-party cookie by name, because those can change when a processor updates its checkout or analytics. Browser settings let you block or delete cookies; blocking them may affect checkout or forms. For how we handle personal information, see the Privacy Policy.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.
