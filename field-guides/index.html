What belongs in this drawer?

A field guide here is a method, not a souvenir. It should answer: where you are standing, what the transport wrote on the stone, which trap or texture is next, and how the specimen keeps its number.

How to use a guide

Ask the land the same four questions.

  1. Whose territory and whose tenure are you standing on?
  2. Did this stone travel, or is it still pointing uphill?
  3. What trap or oxidized cap is concentrating the story?
  4. What number and notes leave the site with the specimen?

If a page cannot help you answer those, it is not a field guide yet — it is still a shop card. Law first. Tests second. Local stones third.
