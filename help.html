Orders

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, add items to your cart, and check out. You'll get a confirmation email right after.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled until they've been produced or shipped. Email support@uniqueperspective8.com with your order number.

I didn't get a confirmation email.

Check your spam folder first. If it isn't there, send us your name and the email you used.

Shipping

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 2 to 5 business days.

Where do you ship?

We ship internationally, but some partner products can't ship to every country, and the product page will say so.

How do I track my order?

We'll email you a tracking link once your order ships.

Who pays international customs fees and duties?

International orders may be subject to customs fees and duties in your country; those are the buyer's responsibility.

Do you offer free shipping?

Orders over $75 CAD qualify for free shipping.

Returns and refunds

What's your return policy?

You can return unused items within 14 days from delivery. See the Refund & Returns Policy.

How do I start a return?

Email support@uniqueperspective8.com with your order number and the reason for the return.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days after we receive the return.

Can I return a custom jewelry order?

Custom jewelry orders are final sale unless there is a defect, as stated in our Policies.

Contact

How do I reach support?

Email support@uniqueperspective8.com. We reply within 2 days.

How do you handle my privacy?

We collect what we need to answer you and complete an order. We do not sell your personal information. Details are on the Privacy Policy page.

Still have questions about the studio or stones? See the FAQ or Contact us.
