Help

Orders, shipping, returns, and how to reach us

Orders

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, add items to your cart, and check out. You'll get a confirmation email right after.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, within 12 hours of ordering. Email customer_support@uniqueperspective8.com with your order number.

I didn't get a confirmation email.

Check your spam folder first. If it isn't there, send us your name and the email you used.

Shipping

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 2 business days. Delivery takes 5 to 7 days after that.

Where do you ship?

We ship worldwide.

How do I track my order?

We'll email you a tracking link once your order ships.

Returns and refunds

What's your return policy?

You can return unused items within 14 days of delivery.

How do I start a return?

Email customer_support@uniqueperspective8.com with your order number and the reason for the return.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days after we receive the return.

Account help

I forgot my password.

Click "Forgot password" on the login page and follow the email link.

How do I update my details?

Sign in and go to your account settings.

Contact

How do I reach support?

Email customer_support@uniqueperspective8.com. We reply within 2 days.

Still have questions about the studio or stones? See the FAQ or Contact us.
