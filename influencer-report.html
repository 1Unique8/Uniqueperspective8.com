Executive Summary

This report identifies YouTube influencer channels best suited for collaboration with Manus AI, the autonomous AI agent that executes tasks end-to-end — from web research and data analysis to website building and workflow automation. The recommended channels were discovered through systematic keyword searches across six content categories, then scored on subscriber scale, geographic relevance, content alignment, and multi-query discovery breadth.

The target audience for Manus AI in the US market spans AI enthusiasts and early adopters, productivity-focused professionals, no-code builders, startup founders, developers, and digital marketers. Channels were selected to cover all these segments, prioritizing US-based creators with established audiences in the 50K–5M subscriber range.

Collaboration Strategy

The report recommends a three-tier partnership model, calibrated to audience size and engagement depth.

Tier 1

Mega & Large Influencers

500K+ subscribers. Channels such as Matt Wolfe, Kevin Stratvert, Futurepedia, Liam Ottley, and AI Revolution. Recommended for sponsored deep-dive reviews, product walkthrough videos, and "day in the life with Manus AI" integrations. Best for broad awareness and product launches.

Tier 2

Mid-Tier Influencers

100K–500K subscribers. Channels like Skill Leap AI, WeAreNoCode, Alicia Lyttle, and Mikey No Code. Highly engaged niche audiences with strong purchase intent. Ideal for tutorial collaborations, use-case demonstrations, and affiliate partnerships.

Tier 3

Micro & Growing Influencers

10K–100K subscribers. Channels such as Jake Dawson, Be Productive, Rob The AI Guy, and Brock Mesarich. Tightly focused audiences with high engagement rates. Ideal for long-term ambassador programs, early access partnerships, and community-building initiatives.
