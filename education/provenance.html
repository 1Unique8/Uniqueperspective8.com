Guide 05 · Paper trail

Field-to-finished provenance

The value of a stone is inseparable from its history. A number assigned in the dirt is worth more than a slogan added at the bench.

Specimen numbering

Significant finds are logged so origin does not depend on memory.

  • Permanent Field ID (UP8-###). Assigned at discovery. Tied to GPS and geological notes.
  • Store listing SKU (UP8-S###). Assigned when the specimen is prepared for the shop or set into jewelry.

Locality series may also carry a place code — for example UP8-PEN-001 for a named stone from a Penticton cut. See the educational cabinet.

What a provenance card carries

  • Discovery date
  • Location: river system or geological formation, not a secret hole on someone else’s claim
  • Geological notes: host rock and mineral associations
  • BC FMC number, when the extraction was done under that standing
  • Artisan signature

Sustainable studio craft

Low-impact beauty, as we mean it: jewelry hand-wrapped in recycled 925 dead-soft sterling silver wire. No synthetic resins, artificial coatings, or stabilizing chemicals that mask the stone’s natural state.

If a piece is drop-shipped, that label belongs on the listing. Studio sentences do not travel onto catalog goods. Read Ethics before you read a romantic caption.
