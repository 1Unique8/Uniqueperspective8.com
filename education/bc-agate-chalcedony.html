One family, two habits

Chalcedony is fibrous microcrystalline quartz. It takes a waxy luster and, in a thin piece, lets light through. Agate is chalcedony that organized itself into bands. This page does not publish coordinates for a hole. It teaches you to recognize the material on lawful ground, and to refuse the name when a dull siltstone only looks wet in the river.

Field tests

Use the five-step protocol. For this family the decisive moments are hardness and edge-light.

  • Hardness. It should scratch glass and ignore a knife.
  • LED translucency. Chalcedony and agate wake. Dense chert and siltstone stay dead.
  • Banding. True agate bands follow the cavity. Metamorphic banding in quartzite is fabric. Compare UP8-PEN-004.
  • Acid. Silica does not fizz.
  • UV. Treat fluorescence as a note, not a verdict.
