Gossan & vein signs

If it is rusty and rotten, gold is not forgotten — but rust is not a certificate. Read the texture before you read the color.

Five signs of a productive hydrothermal quartz vein

  1. Cellular boxwork. Voids where sulfides have leached out, leaving a honeycomb.
  2. Oxidation halos. Brick-red hematite and ochre limonite on the outside of the quartz.
  3. Vuggy textures. Open cavities, often lined with crystal points — space where mineral-rich fluid could move.
  4. Contact selvages. The fringe where vein meets host rock. Often the most mineralized skin.
  5. Heavy accessory minerals. Magnetite, hematite, or dark sulfides inside the quartz body.

A dent test belongs on a loose grain, not on a specimen you intend to keep intact. When in doubt, leave the piece whole and write the uncertainty on the card.
