Guide 02 · Tests, not guesses

Field identification & physical testing

Color lies. Repeatable tests do not. Carry a small kit and write what the stone actually does.

Diagnostic kit

Professional identification is a sequence, not a vibe. Run the same tests in the same order so notes from two days can still talk to each other.

TestToolsWhat it tells you
Mohs hardnessCopper penny (~3.5), steel knife (~5.5), glass plate (~6.5)Separates silicates (often 6.5–7.0) from softer carbonates and alteration minerals.
StreakUnglazed porcelain tileHematite: cherry red-brown. Pyrite: greenish-black. Gold: golden-yellow.
TranslucencyHigh-lumen LED, edge lightingOpaque chert or siltstone versus translucent chalcedony and agate.
UV fluorescence365 nm UV lightSecondary mineralization, calcite veining, regional activator response.
Acid reactionDilute HCl (fizz test)Violent effervescence points to calcite or carbonate matrix. No reaction points to inert silicates.

Handle acid as a field chemical, not a toy. A drop is enough. Neutralize and pack the bottle out. Never test a finished jewelry face.
